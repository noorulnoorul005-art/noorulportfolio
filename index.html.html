Smart Bus Tracker

Mobile App UX/UI Case Study

A mobile bus tracking and ticket booking experience designed to help users find routes, track buses in real time, understand crowd levels, and manage their journey with less uncertainty.

The problem

  • Uncertainty around bus arrival
  • Route information is difficult to find
  • Little awareness of crowd levels
  • Tickets are hard to manage
  • Travel information is fragmented

What the app covers

Case study

Research

A user survey and usability thinking were used to understand how people currently plan and track bus journeys.

Competitive Analysis

Affinity Mapping

Grouping research notes by theme through card sorting to identify the strongest patterns in user needs.
